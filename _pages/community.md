<h1 class="msa-page-title">Strengthening HCI communities across borders</h1>
<p class="msa-intro">Alongside my research and teaching, I contribute to the Asia SIGCHI Committee, the Human-Centered Computing and Society (HCCS) Research Group, and the ACM SIGCHI Dhaka Chapter. These roles involve regional engagement, research-community activities, and scholarly coordination.</p>
