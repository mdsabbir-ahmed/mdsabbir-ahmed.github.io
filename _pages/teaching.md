<p class="msa-eyebrow">TEACHING & MENTORSHIP</p>
<h1 class="msa-page-title">Teaching people, not just computing</h1>
<p class="msa-intro">As a faculty member at BRAC University, I teach introductory through advanced computer science courses and mentor students undertaking thesis and publication-oriented research. My teaching spans technical foundations, computational thinking, and human-centered approaches to AI and computing.</p>
<div class="msa-stack">
<article class="msa-row-feature"><span class="msa-index">ADVANCED</span><div><h2>Human-Computer Interaction & Artificial Intelligence</h2><p><strong>CSE472:</strong> Human-Computer Interaction (laboratory) · <strong>CSE422:</strong> Artificial Intelligence · <strong>CSE427:</strong> Machine Learning · <strong>CSE437:</strong> Data Science.</p><p>Topics include user research, evaluative methods, responsible computational approaches, and developing evidence-based analyses.</p></div></article>
<article class="msa-row-feature"><span class="msa-index">CORE</span><div><h2>Foundational Computer Science</h2><p><strong>CSE110:</strong> Programming Language I · <strong>CSE111:</strong> Programming Language II · additional earlier teaching in computer networks, introductory CS, numerical methods, and systems analysis.</p><p>Course and laboratory coordination, assessment design, consultation, and student-facing instructional resources have been recurring parts of my teaching role.</p></div></article>
<article class="msa-row-feature"><span class="msa-index">MENTORSHIP</span><div><h2>Research supervision and academic development</h2><p>I have supervised or co-supervised more than 50 undergraduate thesis and project groups, supporting topic selection, research design, execution, interpretation, and scholarly writing. I also contribute to student research groups and faculty-induction activities.</p></div></article>
</div>
<h2>Educational leadership</h2>
<p>My institutional responsibilities include Undergraduate Program Coordination, Student Tutor Program Coordination, freshers' academic advising, and support for faculty induction through BRAC University's Learning and Teaching Innovation Centre. These are complementary to, rather than the primary focus of, my research profile.</p>
