    <h1>Human-centered research on <span>trust, technology, and society.</span></h1>
    <p class="msa-hero-lead">I am <strong>Md. Sabbir Ahmed</strong>, a Senior Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at <a href="https://www.bracu.ac.bd/">BRAC University</a>, currently based in Sydney, Australia. My work explores how people understand, trust, and navigate digital and AI-mediated systems, particularly where information, safety, and power intersect.</p>
    <p class="msa-hero-sub">My research brings together <strong>Human-Computer Interaction</strong>, <strong>Human-Centered and Trustworthy AI</strong>, <strong>Usable Security and Privacy</strong>, and <strong>Social Computing</strong>, with an emphasis on underserved communities in South Asia and emerging applications in health and education.</p>
