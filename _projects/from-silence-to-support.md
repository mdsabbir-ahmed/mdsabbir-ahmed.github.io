<h1 class="msa-page-title">From Silence to Support (S2S)</h1>
<p class="msa-intro">Co-designing a human-led, AI-assisted digital mental health support pathway for university students in Bangladesh.</p>
<div class="msa-note"><strong>Role:</strong> Principal Investigator</div>
**From Silence to Support (S2S)** is a funded interdisciplinary project studying why university students do not always seek support when experiencing distress and how a culturally situated support ecosystem might respond.

### Vision

The project investigates a **human-led, AI-assisted peer-support pathway**. Rather than treating a chatbot as a replacement for mental health professionals or trusted personal support, AI is considered for a bounded first-contact role within a broader, safer system.

### Research program

- **Study 1:** Mixed-methods needs assessment of help-seeking barriers and informal use of large language models for emotional support.
- **Later studies:** Participatory co-design followed by evaluation of safety, trustworthiness, usability, and cultural fit.

**Funding:** BRAC University Research Seed Grant Initiative (RSGI), BDT 600,000. **Role:** Principal Investigator.

*Status: Ongoing research. No intervention effectiveness claims are made at this stage.*
