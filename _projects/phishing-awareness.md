<h1 class="msa-page-title">Beyond the Bait</h1>
<p class="msa-intro">Funded project exploring gamified awareness interventions for phishing and SMiShing threats in Bangladesh.</p>
<div class="msa-note"><strong>Role:</strong> Co-Principal Investigator</div>
**Beyond the Bait** explores intervention design for phishing and SMS-based phishing (SMiShing) awareness in Bangladesh's evolving cybersecurity landscape.

This work centers usable security, risk perception, and approaches to helping users recognize and respond to deceptive communication.

**Role:** Co-Principal Investigator. **Funding:** BRAC University Research Seed Grant Initiative, BDT 500,000.
