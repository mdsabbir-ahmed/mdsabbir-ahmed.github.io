<h1 class="msa-page-title">Information, Distress, and Diffused Surveillance</h1>
<p class="msa-intro">A mixed-methods investigation of trust, information behavior, distress, and digital surveillance during Bangladesh's July 2024 political movement.</p>
<div class="msa-note"><strong>Role:</strong> Research contributor / co-first author of related publication</div>
This research examined the digital information environment surrounding Bangladesh's July 2024 political movement, including information sharing, verification, perceived surveillance, and psychological distress. The mixed-methods study combined a survey of **1,162 participants** with **23 semi-structured interviews**.

### Research questions and contribution

How do people continue to communicate and participate when digital information may be unreliable and online visibility may carry consequences? The work approaches surveillance and resilience as socially situated practices rather than solely individual technological decisions.

### Research outcomes

A related co-first-authored paper, **Resilience Amid Diffused Surveillance During a Political Movement in Bangladesh**, appeared at **USENIX Security 2026**. Related quantitative analyses are being developed separately and are not represented as published outcomes.
