<h1 class="msa-page-title">Privacy and Accessibility in AI-Integrated Health Records</h1>
<p class="msa-intro">Funded research on privacy, patient accessibility, and predictive analytics for integrated health record management.</p>

The description reflects the funded research agenda; no clinical validation or deployment results are claimed.
