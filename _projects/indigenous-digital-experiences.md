<h1 class="msa-page-title">Indigenous Digital Experiences and Knowledge Infrastructures</h1>
<p class="msa-intro">Research on social media use, privacy, online support, and unequal digital infrastructures among Indigenous communities in Bangladesh.</p>
<div class="msa-note"><strong>Role:</strong> Research collaborator / co-investigator</div>
This line of work examines technology use and participation among Indigenous communities in Bangladesh, including how digital opportunities interact with privacy, safety, recognition, and unequal access to knowledge infrastructures.

### Published foundation

Our **CHI 2025** publication, *The Good, The Bad and The Ugly*, investigates opportunities, challenges, and mitigation strategies related to social media use among young Indigenous people in the Chittagong Hill Tracts.

### Subsequent research

An associated funded study addresses online privacy, digital security, and digital self-expression among Indigenous communities. Separate work considers mental health and unequal knowledge infrastructures. Findings from manuscripts still under review are not reported as published findings here.

[CHI 2025 publication](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713268)
