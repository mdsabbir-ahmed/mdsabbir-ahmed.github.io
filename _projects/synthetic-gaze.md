<p class="msa-intro">Multi-country South Asian research on how people trust, recognize, and share AI-generated content and deepfakes.</p>
<div class="msa-note"><strong>Role:</strong> Bangladesh country representative / collaborator</div>

I contribute to research instrument development and coordinate the Bangladesh study component.
